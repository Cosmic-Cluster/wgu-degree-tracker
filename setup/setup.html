WGU Program Tracker · Setup

Build your tracker from your Program Guide

Load your program's Program Guide PDF, check what was read, adjust your plan, and download a config.js for the tracker. Everything happens in this page — your PDF is never uploaded anywhere.
Step 1

Load your Program Guide

Download it from your program's page on wgu.edu (look for "Program Guide"), or from your student portal. Use the version for your catalog year if you can.
Step 2

Check what was found

Step 3

Your plan

How should courses be split into terms?
Changing this re-assigns every course's term below, replacing any terms you typed by hand.
Step 4

Courses

In the order they appear in the guide. Fix anything that was misread, tick Transfer for courses already covered by transfer credit, and change any course's term. Course codes are optional — most guides don't list them.

Mark in-person courses from your program's web page (optional)
Program Guides often don't say which courses have in-person clinicals or labs, but your program's page on wgu.edu may. Copy its course list (including any footnotes like "*This course includes … in-person clinical requirements") and paste it here. Only the In person column and notes change. Check the result before downloading.
Step 5

Program requirements (not courses)

Exams, licensure steps, clearances and anything else your program needs that isn't a course. Clinicals, student teaching and portfolios are usually real courses with CUs, so they appear in the course list above instead. Anything the guide mentioned is pre-filled; untick what doesn't apply to you.
Step 6

Download

  1. Move the downloaded config.js into the main tracker folder, next to index.html. Replace the old one if there is one.
  2. Open index.html.
Re-running setup later keeps your saved progress for any course whose name (or code) didn't change.
